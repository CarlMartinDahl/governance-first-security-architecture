```text
https://github.com/CarlMartinDahl/governance-first-security-architecture

endorsement by Martin Dahl or the canonical project.
```

Equivalent attribution is acceptable when the medium requires another format, provided that it identifies Martin Dahl, names the project, links to the canonical source and license where reasonably practicable, identifies material changes, and does not imply endorsement.
